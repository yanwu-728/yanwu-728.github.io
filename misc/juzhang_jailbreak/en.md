I came home from my Sunday morning workout class to a disaster zone: our heavy cat tree toppled onto the shoe rack, scattering our sneakers and slippers across the entryway. It was clearly Juzhang's doing, though I had just waved goodbye to him an hour earlier.

I kept calling his name until my boyfriend Nick woke up and pointed out that the window screen was pushed open. Even though Juzhang spent probably two years surviving on the street before we adopted him, he has been a thoroughly spoiled indoor cat ever since. Panic set in and I ran out the door looking for him.

### Cat around the corner

The search didn't last very long. Juzhang was right around the corner, casually chilling on the sidewalk, and walked up to me nonchalantly. As germaphobic as Nick has always been, he scooped the cat up and headed directly to the bathroom for a bath.

However, the moment Nick turned on the faucet, he noticed blood. We shut the water off and did a full inspection: a cut behind his left ear, one on his nose, chipped nails, and scratched paws. Seems like he got into some trouble while being outside.

### Sunday emergency vet

Bath time was canceled. We started looking for a Sunday emergency vet that was reasonably close by and didn't have terrible Google reviews.

![Juzhang at vet](assets/images/juzhang_vet.jpg)

Aside from getting spooked by a couple of barking dogs in the waiting room, Juzhang truly made himself right at home the moment he was released from the carrier. He walked around the exam room, sniffed every doctor and nurse's hand, and meowed non-stop. He also behaved remarkably well while the vet took his blood and gave him booster shots, though the doctor did trip over him when he suddenly appeared under her desk.

The vet was certain that Juzhang got into a fight with another animal—perhaps the very reason he ran out in the first place. We'll probably never know what he tangled with, but judging from the pure pride in his walk when he marched back, I'm pretty sure he won.
