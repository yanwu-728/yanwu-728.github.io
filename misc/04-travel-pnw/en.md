### Pacific Northwest Summer Road Trip

Road trips around Seattle and Washington state between graduating MIT undergrad and starting grad school:

- **Mount Rainier**: Hiking Skyline Trail with panoramic views of glaciers, alpine meadows, and wildflowers.
- **Olympic National Park**: Exploring the lush mossy trails of the Hoh Rain Forest and rugged Pacific coastlines.
- **Seattle**: Waterfront strolls, Pike Place Market, coffee roasteries, and ferry rides across Puget Sound.
