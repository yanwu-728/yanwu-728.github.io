### Navigating F-1 OPT to H-1B

Notes and practical experiences transitioning from MIT student status to full-time engineering in the US:

- **Timeline Planning**: Applying for post-completion OPT up to 90 days before graduation to ensure smooth authorization start dates.
- **Cap-Gap & H-1B Registration**: Coordinating with company immigration counsel on the annual lottery and Cap-Gap extensions.
- **Documentation**: Keeping I-20s, EADs, and travel signatures organized across interstate relocations.
