Hanging out with Juzhang (局长, pronounced *"joo-jahng"*—literally 'bureau chief'), my orange 4-year-old cat who faithfully oversees all coding sprints, problem-solving, and nap schedules.

He has a strict policy on code quality: if a bug takes too long to fix, he steps directly onto the keyboard to suggest immediate debugging breaks. When not auditing terminal output, he is either demanding churu treats or claiming the warmest sunbeam in the apartment.
